## Course Description

Introductory computer science through exploration and analysis of mathematical puzzles and games, and the algorithms that handle them.

## QD: Quantitative and Data Literacy 

Since data analysis drives research in academic disciplines and decision-making in applied contexts, it is critical that students have experience manipulating and drawing conclusions from data sets. QD-designated courses demonstrate and apply quantitative approaches within a disciplinary context, ensuring that students are able to extract meaning from data-rich information and to apply appropriate analytical tools in assessing that meaning. 

## Learning Objectives

* To engage in productive struggle and data collection to solve common puzzles and understand common mathematical games through computational thinking
* To understand, appreciate, and communicate the various approaches and algorithms to solving the games and puzzles
* To connect the puzzles and games to real-world applications and transfer the knowledge and understanding to help shape real-world decisions and actions
* QD Objectives:
  * Present data in a variety of ways, including analytical, graphical, and tabular. 
  * Interpret data, solve problems, and draw conclusions from data presented in a variety of ways, including analytical, graphical, and tabular, and communicate the thought processes involved.
  * Evaluate data-rich information and determine whether the resulting conclusions make logical sense or support a given argument within the context provided.
