## Course Description

You will explore the ethical dilemmas and societal impacts of computer technologies.
You will research and write about current events related to technology and society.
You will present a topic and participate in discussion groups in class.
You will participate in service that helps someone outside of CS learn about technology.

## Learning Objectives

* You will look at the history of computer milestones and important contributions of individuals or groups (AOE CSES 3.2.1-3.2.2) to understand where we currently stand with technology and where we can go next.
* You will discuss and write about the ethical acquisition and use of information, intellectual property rights, equitable use of technology, digital citizenship, online threats, and other areas at the intersection of technology and society (AOE CSES 3.8.1-3.8.5).

## Grading

Points system:

* 50 pts - Discussion class attendance and reflection papers (n=10, 5 pts each)
* 20 pts - Prepare and present a topic and lead class discussion in a team (n=1)
* 20 pts - Service experience and essay (n=1)
* 20 pts - Other assignments throughout the semester (n=4, 5 pts each)

This means there will be at least 110 points available throughout the semester. Your final grade will be based on a 100-point scale, so you will have some wiggle room built in.

**Discussion reflection papers**: After each discussion class, you will have one week to submit a one-page reflection paper on Gradescope. This should not be notes taken from class, but rather your reactions, contemplations, elaborations, and thoughts about the discussion material.

**Discussion presentation**: Presentations days and topics will be chosen the first day of class. You will meet with your team and the instructor for the last 5 minutes of class the week before your presentation to solidify the pre-readings and to make sure you have a plan. Then you will lead a discussion during the next class period. More details on the format of the discussion are posted in Brightspace.

**Service experience and essay**: The service experience should be at least one hour long (it doesn’t have to be in one sitting). For the service experience to qualify, you need to volunteer your time to teach someone something they didn’t previously know and that knowledge has to be related to technology. The service experience has to be done sometime during the current semester. You will then write a 2-page essay detailing what you did for the service, who you helped, your reflections on the experience, and whether or not you would do it again.

**Other assignments**: The instructor may ask you to complete other tasks at any point.


## Assessment

* Discussion reflection papers will be graded out of 5 points:
  * 1 point for attending the discussion class period
  * 4 points for your reflections on the topics discussed. What biases or expectations did you have before class? Which discussion points resonated with you most? Have your opinions changed on any topic? Is there a topic that you had never thought about before?
* Discussion presentation will be graded out of 20 points:
  * 18 points based on your preparation and participation in your team's discussion day
  * 2 points based on peer review post-class
* Service experience will be graded based on your participation in the activity and your reflection essay.
* Other assignments will be graded based on completion.

## Accommodations

In keeping with University policy, any student with a documented disability interested in utilizing ADA accommodations should contact Student Accessibility Services (SAS), the office of Disability Services on campus for students.
